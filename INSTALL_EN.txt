WHALE — Mastering
=================

INSTALLATION
------------
macOS   : double-click WHALE-x.y.z-macOS.pkg (VST3 + AU).
          If macOS blocks the installer: right-click > Open, or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Windows : run WHALE-x.y.z-Windows-Setup.exe (VST3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3).

Then rescan your plug-ins:
  - Ableton Live : Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan.
  - FL Studio    : Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
  - Logic Pro    : the AU version is picked up on the next launch.

QUICK START
-----------
1. Put WHALE LAST on the master bus.
2. Play the loudest part of the track (drop / chorus).
3. Click AUTO and let it play: WHALE listens for 30 seconds (it keeps the loudest parts), sets the chain,
   then measures the processed sound for about 30 seconds and fine-tunes what is left.
   The report shows the section it listened to (e.g. "1:02 to 1:32") and warns you if it contained a break:
   then run AUTO again during a drop. If the style was a close call, the caption shows "(close to …)":
   pick the right style in STYLE and run AUTO again.
4. Compare with BEFORE / AFTER (matched loudness on the loud parts: you hear the real difference, not just "louder").
   The meters on the right show what you hear: TRUE PEAK, TONE (distance to the style's references),
   DYNAMICS (green = not squashed for your target), STEREO, MONO BASS.
   Not convinced? "↶ UNDO AUTO" (top left, or menu ≡) brings back exactly the settings you had before.
5. Adjust with the 8 knobs (0 = AUTO's choice), or pick a preset.
6. EXPERT: detailed settings for every module + AUTO REPORT (what was done, and why).

The whale rises to the surface (the loudness target) while mastering, doubles up if there is a phase problem,
turns orange when it is too loud, and blows stars when the master is validated.
Menu ≡ > Reduced animations for slower computers.

LICENSE
-------
WHALE is fully usable for 7 days (the "TRIAL" counter at the top left). After that, paste the license key you
received by e-mail after your purchase: click "TRIAL" / "ACTIVATE", or menu ≡ > License. An internet connection
is needed only once, for activation. One key works on 5 computers.
Without a license after the trial, WHALE mutes the sound for 2 seconds every 40 seconds.
